What Business Central covers

  • Financial management

    General ledger, AP/AR, fixed assets, multi-entity consolidation, cash flow forecasting, and dimensional reporting.

  • Supply chain and inventory

    Demand planning, purchase orders, lot tracking, serial numbers, warehouse management, and real-time inventory across locations.

  • Project management

    Time and expense tracking, job costing, resource allocation, and project budgeting with real-time margin visibility.

  • Sales and service

    Opportunity management, quotes, order processing, returns, and customer service. Native sales order management or full Dynamics 365 Sales integration.

  • Manufacturing

    Production orders, detailed BOMs, support for multiple manufacturing types. Capacity planning and shop floor management available with the Premium license.

  • Integration and extensibility

    Open API, pre-built connectors, Power Platform, Power BI, Power Automate and access to the full Microsoft 365 ecosystem including Copilot AI.
